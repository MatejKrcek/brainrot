# Brain Health — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 27 September 2026_

Brain Health is an iOS app by Matěj Krček that shows how much time you spend in the apps you choose and turns it into a picture of a brain.

## What the app collects

**Nothing leaves your iPhone.** Brain Health has no server, no account, no analytics and no advertising SDK. It makes no network requests.

## Screen Time data

If you allow Screen Time access, Apple's Screen Time framework (FamilyControls, DeviceActivity, ManagedSettings) reports how many minutes you have spent in the apps you selected. Apple enforces that this data is processed only on your device: the app itself only receives minute counts, and exact per-app numbers are rendered by a sandboxed Apple extension that cannot share them with anything else.

The app stores on your device, in a container shared only with its own widgets and extensions:

- today's minute count and a per-day history of minute counts,
- the apps and categories you selected,
- your daily limit and blocking preferences.

You can delete all of it at any time with **Settings → Reset all data**, or by deleting the app.

## Blocking

If you turn on blocking, the app uses Apple's ManagedSettings to show a pause screen over your selected apps when you pass your limit. This works entirely on the device.

## Children

Brain Health is not directed at children and does not use Screen Time's family/parental mode; it only ever requests individual authorisation for the device's own user.
